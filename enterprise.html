How it works

Your strategy is a folder, not a SaaS contract.

Workboard locks your strategy inside a closed database under a SaaS contract. Cabinet stores it the other way around: your OKRs, org chart, decisions, one-on-ones and meetings live as roughly 200 plain text files in a folder you control. Version-controlled by git. Hosted wherever you choose.

Everything in one place

OKRs, org chart, decisions, meetings: connected, kept up to date by the AI teammates.

Versioned by git

Every change is a commit. Roll back a quarter, branch a strategy, diff a roadmap.

Humans and AI read the same files

Plain markdown. No proprietary index. Nothing hidden from you.

Your cloud or ours

Run on each manager's laptop, in your VPC, or on Cabinet Cloud. Your choice.
