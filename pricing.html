Questions

Cabinet is open source. Why would I pay?

You don't have to. Running it yourself is free forever. Paying gets us to run it for you instead, so you don't have to manage a server, updates, or backups.

Is my AI cost included in the price?

No. Connect the OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini account your company already has, and that stays a separate bill. Don't have one? We can sell you AI access too, starting at $10 a month.

Can I switch from paid hosting back to running it myself?

Yes, anytime. Download your data and move it to your own server. Nothing is locked in.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you're on. Annual plans can be refunded in the first 30 days.
