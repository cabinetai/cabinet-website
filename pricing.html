Pricing questions

The details buyers usually need.

The short version: self-hosting stays free, Cabinet Cloud is optional, and the company keeps control of its AI relationships and work.

Cabinet is open source. Why would I pay?

You do not have to. Self-hosted Cabinet is free and includes the complete product. Cabinet Cloud is for teams that want Cabinet available around the clock without running the server, updates, and backups themselves.

Can I move from Cabinet Cloud back to self-hosted?

Yes. Export the whole Cabinet directory and run it in your own environment. Your knowledge, files, apps, agents, and work history move together.

Where does my data live?

Self-hosted Cabinet lives wherever you deploy it. Cabinet Cloud uses encrypted storage on its managed container. Region pinning is available on Max and Enterprise.

How are AI costs handled?

Bring your own AI is the default on every plan. Connect the OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, or other provider access your company already manages. Managed AI starts at $10 per month if you prefer one invoice.

Is there a free Cabinet Cloud trial?

Self-hosted Cabinet is the free evaluation path and uses the same product. Cabinet Cloud is currently on a waitlist while the managed service is prepared for broader access.

Does Cabinet support teams?

Cabinet is single-user today. Shared workspaces are entering the Enterprise track first, with a self-serve Team plan in early access.

What does data protection include on Max?

Max includes encrypted backups, 30-day retention, point-in-time restore, and region pinning. Pro includes daily backups with 7-day retention.

Can I cancel Cabinet Cloud?

Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refundable during the first 30 days and non-refundable after that window.
