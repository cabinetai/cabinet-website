About

Gmail for Business

A front desk for your business inbox. Every morning at 7 it pulls out:

  • Waiting on you: leads and customers who need an answer, most urgent first, with replies already drafted in your voice.
  • Quotes with no answer: the quotes you sent that nobody has said yes to yet, with a friendly nudge ready.
  • Gone quiet: regular customers you have not heard from in a while, worth a call.

Write an email from the page: write it yourself, or tell the agent what to say. It goes out only when you press Send on it in the chat.

Newsletters and ads are set aside and counted. The Ideas on the front desk go further: list every new lead this week, build your customer list from your email, or send your partner a weekly summary. Each one is a tap, now or on a schedule.

Invoices, payments and bills live in Gmail for Finance.

To start

Open the Front desk and press Connect Gmail. It takes a minute or two, and Cabinet shows you each step. Until then the front desk shows a made-up business, clearly marked.

For now Gmail works in the Cabinet app on your computer. It can't be connected on Cabinet Cloud yet.

It only reads

Your front desk never sends, deletes or moves anything in Gmail. When you want to answer, it writes the reply in the chat, and nothing goes out without your OK.

Inside

  • Front desk: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about the business.
  • Reports: one page per morning, the Friday list of who is still waiting, the Monday list of regulars who went quiet, and the lists you ask for, like your customer list.
