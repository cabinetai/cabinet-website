About

Text Your Mom

Text Your Mom is a playful consumer app that helps people stay close to family and friends by nudging them to reply, check in, and keep tiny relationship promises before avoidance turns into guilt.

Why This Example Works

  • Everybody knows the feeling of meaning to reply "later" and realizing it has been five days.
  • The product is emotionally real, funny, and easy to explain.
  • It gives the company a natural growth loop across TikTok, Reddit, and app product work.

Active Root Team

  • CEO for strategic alignment, executive communication, and subscription economics & runway
  • COO for operational reviews, process health, and technical direction & reliability

Child Cabinets

  • marketing/tiktok for short-form acquisition and creative testing
  • marketing/reddit for community insight, post experiments, and comment capture
  • app-development for roadmap, PRDs, quality, and release work

The root company cabinet is active. The child cabinets are installed as imported examples and remain paused until reviewed.
