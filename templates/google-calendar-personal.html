About

Calendar for Personal

Your own week, from your Google Calendar. It shows what is on today, the appointments and plans coming up, the birthdays to remember, and when you are free in the evenings and at the weekend. It reads your calendar every morning at 7, and the page keeps time all day.

The cabinet opens on it: My week.

What you get

  • Today: what is on now, what is next with a live countdown, and the rest of the day.
  • Coming up: appointments, bookings, school dates and trips in the next 30 days.
  • Birthdays and dates: who to celebrate soon, with a nudge to plan something.
  • Send my free times: your free evenings and weekend times as a message for friends or family, ready to copy.
  • This week: your seven days, as a list by day or as a real week view.
  • Add to your calendar: fill in a new event, or tell the agent in your own words what to add or move. A new event waits for your press on its card in the chat; a move or a delete waits for your yes.

Every Sunday evening it writes the week ahead: each day's plans, and what to prepare.

Press Edit layout to add, remove, move and resize the boxes. Your layout is yours.

For work meetings and clients, use Calendar for Business.

Getting started

  1. On the page, press Connect Google Calendar. You do it once.
  2. A minute later your own week is there. It updates every morning at 7.

Until then, the page shows a made-up week, and says so.

It only reads, unless you ask

The morning update never adds, moves or deletes anything, and never sends anything. If you ask in the chat to move an event, it says exactly what will change and waits for your yes.

Inside

  • My week: the app this cabinet opens on.
  • Reports: the week ahead every Sunday, and the lists you ask for.
