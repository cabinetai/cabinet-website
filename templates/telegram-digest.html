About

Telegram Digest

Telegram shows every chat. This shows the few that need you, and what you can safely skip.

Every morning at 9, your digest reads the chats and groups your Cabinet bot is in and opens on one page:

  • Since the last morning: a message from cabinet with the one thing that matters, and every message as a small dot, the ones that need you in blue.
  • Waiting on you: people who asked you, mentioned you or replied to you, longest wait first, each with a reply in your voice to copy.
  • You said you would: what you told people you would do, found in your own messages. Telegram never reminds you; this does.
  • Decided in your groups: plans and choices, one line each, with who agreed.
  • Links and files: what people sent you, so you can find it again.
  • Channels: each channel that posted, and whether it is worth reading.
  • Safe to skip: the loud chats that never needed you, counted, so you can trust the rest.

Ask anything about your chats in the box at the bottom, try an idea ("Catch me up on a busy group", "Every plan in one list"), and arrange the page your way with Layout.

To start

Open Catch up: it starts with a setup guide, ten short steps with a picture of each screen. Press Connect Telegram. BotFather makes you a bot in about two minutes, and you paste its key into Cabinet. Add the bot to the groups you want read: it sees a group from the moment it joins. To let it see every message in a group, not only the ones that mention it, send BotFather /setprivacy, pick your bot and choose Disable. The first reading takes about a minute and shows you what it finds as it reads. Until then the page shows a made-up Telegram, clearly marked.

It only reads

It never sends, replies, reacts or marks anything as read. Replies are yours to copy and send.

Inside

  • Catch up: the app this cabinet opens on, and what you told it about your chats.
  • Chat Reader: the agent. Reads your Telegram, writes the page, answers in the chat.
  • Morning Telegram Digest: the routine, every day at 9.
