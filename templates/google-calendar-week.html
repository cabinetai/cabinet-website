About

Calendar for Business

Your work week with clients, from your Google Calendar. It shows what is on now and next, helps you before and after every client meeting, and keeps track of the people you work with. It reads your calendar every morning at 7, and the page keeps time all day.

The cabinet opens on it: Your week.

What you get

  • Today: the meeting on now, the next one with a live countdown, and the rest of your working day.
  • Before and after: meetings to prepare for, with a short brief ready, and follow-ups to send after them.
  • Send my free times: your open times this week as a short message, ready to copy into an email when someone asks when you are free.
  • This week: your seven days, as a list by day or as a real week view.
  • Clients you meet: who you met, how often, and when you meet them next. Going quiet names the clients you have not seen in a while, with a check-in ready.
  • Double bookings: meetings that overlap, with help to fix them.
  • Add to your calendar: fill in a new event, or tell the agent in your own words what to add or move. A new event waits for your press on its card in the chat; a move or a delete waits for your yes.

Press Edit layout to add, remove, move and resize the boxes. Your layout is yours.

For your own life (family, school, appointments), use Calendar for Personal. For hours you bill to clients, use Calendar for Freelancers.

Getting started

  1. On the page, press Connect Google Calendar. You do it once.
  2. A minute later your own week is there. It updates every morning at 7.

Until then, the page shows a made-up week, and says so.

It only reads, unless you ask

The morning update never adds, moves or deletes anything, and never sends anything. Drafts are written for you to send yourself. If you ask in the chat to move an event, it says exactly what will change and waits for your yes. Connected with your calendar's secret address, it can read your week but not change it.
