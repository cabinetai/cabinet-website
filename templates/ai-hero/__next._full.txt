- **💻 Code Tutor** — reviews your notebook attempts; explains PyTorch + Python patterns

## Ground rules

1. **Do exercises yourself.** Wrong attempts teach more than copying a solution.
2. **Type the code.** Do not copy-paste from Karpathy's videos. Your fingers need the reps.
3. **Write in the notes fields.** Each section has a "My notes" block. Future-you needs them.
4. **If you're stuck > 30 min**, ask the tutors — don't ask ChatGPT (it'll give you spoilers).

<p><strong>~20 hours</strong> of focused work. Plan ahead — do it in small chunks so things sink in. Interactive webapps, embedded videos, and live notebooks make every concept something you can poke at, not just read about.</p>

<h2>Your team</h2>
<p>Two agents live in this cabinet — ask either anything. They are <strong>not ChatGPT</strong>: do not ask them to do your exercises for you. They are study partners, not spoiler machines.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>🎓 Mentor</strong> — plans your week, sets pace, flags when you're behind, explains math intuitions (matrix ops, chain rule, probability), and owns <code>progress/progress.csv</code> + the dashboard</li>
<li><strong>💻 Code Tutor</strong> — reviews your notebook attempts; explains PyTorch + Python patterns</li>
</ul>
<h2>Ground rules</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Do exercises yourself.</strong> Wrong attempts teach more than copying a solution.</li>
<li><strong>Type the code.</strong> Do not copy-paste from Karpathy's videos. Your fingers need the reps.</li>
<li><strong>Write in the notes fields.</strong> Each section has a "My notes" block. Future-you needs them.</li>
<li><strong>If you're stuck > 30 min</strong>, ask the tutors — don't ask ChatGPT (it'll give you spoilers).</li>
</ol>
