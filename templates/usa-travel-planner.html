About

🇺🇸 USA Travel Planner

An inspiration board for exploring America. All 63 national parks on a clickable map, every state fair, every federal holiday, the iconic regional fast-food chain in each state — plus an agent that scans X and news every week for cool stuff happening right now.

🗺️ Where to next

Your team

Two agents. Ask either anything in the AI panel.

  • 🧭 Travel Curator — plans trips, stitches multi-stop itineraries, balances your bucket list against your calendar. Also covers national parks (trails, seasons, permits), regional food (iconic chains by state, BBQ regions), and holidays (where to be for each one).
  • 📡 Event Hunter — scans X / news / Reddit / Eventbrite weekly, appends finds to the [[inspiration/index|Inspiration Feed]]

Pick your starting point

Got a weekend? Open [[parks-map/index|the parks map]] and find the closest one you haven't been to. Got two weeks? Ask the Travel Curator: "Plan a 14-day road trip from [your city] hitting 3 parks I haven't visited." Just browsing? Read the [[inspiration/index|Inspiration Feed]] — that's where the Event Hunter posts what's actually cool right now. Hungry? [[fast-food/index|Fast Food]] — pick a state, find your meal.
